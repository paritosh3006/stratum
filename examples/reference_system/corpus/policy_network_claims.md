# Arogya Suraksha — Network Hospitals, Sub-limits and Grievances

## Network hospitals

A network hospital is a hospital that has entered into an agreement with the
company or its third party administrator for cashless treatment. The current
list of network hospitals is published on the company website and is subject to
change without notice.

Treatment at a non-network hospital is payable on a reimbursement basis subject
to the same terms as network treatment.

## Sub-limits

Cataract surgery is subject to a sub-limit of 40000 per eye.

Knee replacement surgery is subject to a sub-limit of 200000 per joint.

Modern treatment methods, including robotic surgery, stem cell therapy and oral
chemotherapy, are payable up to 50 percent of the sum insured.

Consumables and non-medical items listed in Annexure III are not payable and
must be borne by the insured.

## Co-payment

For members aged 61 years and above at first entry, a co-payment of 20 percent
applies to every admissible claim. The co-payment is applied after the deduction
of non-payable items.

No co-payment applies to members who entered the policy before the age of 61 and
have maintained continuous cover.

## Cumulative bonus

A cumulative bonus of 10 percent of the sum insured is added for every claim-free
policy year, up to a maximum of 50 percent. The cumulative bonus is reduced by 10
percent for each policy year in which a claim is paid.

The cumulative bonus does not attract additional premium.

## Grievance redressal

A grievance may be registered with the customer service department by telephone
or in writing. The company will acknowledge a grievance within three working days
and resolve it within 15 days.

If the response is not satisfactory, the insured may approach the grievance
redressal officer whose details are printed on the policy schedule.

If the grievance remains unresolved for 30 days, the insured may approach the
Insurance Ombudsman having jurisdiction over the insured's place of residence.
The Ombudsman may be approached for claims up to a value of 3000000.

## Claim status notifications

Claim status updates are sent by SMS using the template: Your claim {claim_id}
has been {status} for an approved amount of {amount}.

Policy renewal reminders are sent by email using the template: Your policy
{policy_number} is due for renewal on {renewal_date}.
