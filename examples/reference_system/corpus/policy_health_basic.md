# Arogya Suraksha Health Insurance — Policy Wording

## 1. Preamble

This policy is a contract between the insured and the company. Cover is subject
to the terms, conditions and exclusions stated in this wording and in the policy
schedule. The premium is payable in advance and the policy is renewable annually
subject to the company's underwriting.

## 2. Definitions

**Premium** means the amount payable by the policyholder to keep this policy in
force, as stated in the policy schedule.

**Sum insured** means the maximum amount payable under this policy during one
policy year, as stated in the policy schedule.

**Waiting period** means the continuous period from the commencement of the
first policy during which specified benefits are not payable.

**Pre-existing disease** means any condition, ailment or injury diagnosed by a
physician within 48 months prior to the first policy issued by the company.

## 3. Waiting periods

An initial waiting period of 30 days applies from the commencement of the first
policy. Claims for any illness contracted during these 30 days are not payable.
This waiting period does not apply to hospitalisation arising from accident.

Specified surgical procedures carry a waiting period of 24 months from the
commencement of the first policy. These include knee replacement surgery, hip
replacement surgery, cataract surgery, hernia repair, and surgery for benign
prostatic hypertrophy.

Pre-existing diseases are covered after a continuous waiting period of 36 months
from the commencement of the first policy with the company.

Maternity expenses are payable after a waiting period of 24 months of continuous
coverage. The maternity benefit is limited to two deliveries during the lifetime
of the policy.

## 4. Covered expenses

Hospitalisation expenses are payable where the insured is admitted for a minimum
of 24 consecutive hours. The 24-hour minimum does not apply to day-care
procedures listed in Annexure II, which include cataract surgery, chemotherapy,
dialysis and lithotripsy.

Pre-hospitalisation expenses incurred up to 60 days before admission are
payable, provided the expenses relate to the condition for which the insured was
hospitalised.

Post-hospitalisation expenses incurred up to 90 days after discharge are payable
on the same basis.

Ambulance charges are payable up to 2000 per hospitalisation event.

Room rent is payable up to 1 percent of the sum insured per day for a shared
room and 2 percent of the sum insured per day for a single private room.

Physiotherapy is payable when prescribed by the treating physician as part of
post-hospitalisation recovery, up to a limit of 15 sessions per policy year.

Cardiac procedures including bypass surgery and angioplasty are payable after
the 24-month waiting period for specified procedures has elapsed.

Chemotherapy and radiotherapy are payable as day-care procedures without the
24-hour hospitalisation requirement.

## 5. Exclusions

Cosmetic surgery is excluded unless it forms part of reconstruction following an
accident or cancer surgery, and is certified as medically necessary by the
treating physician.

Dental treatment is excluded unless it arises from an accidental injury
requiring hospitalisation.

Expenses arising from participation in hazardous activities, including
mountaineering, scuba diving and motor racing, are excluded.

Treatment taken outside India is not covered under this policy.

Any expense arising from a condition for which the insured had signs or symptoms
before the commencement of the first policy and which was not disclosed is
excluded.

## 6. Claim procedure

For cashless treatment the insured must obtain pre-authorisation from the third
party administrator at least 48 hours before a planned admission, or within 24
hours of an emergency admission.

For reimbursement claims, the claim form and supporting documents must be
submitted within 30 days of discharge. Required documents include the discharge
summary, original bills, investigation reports and the treating physician's
prescription.

The company will settle or reject a claim within 30 days of receiving the last
necessary document. Where an investigation is required, settlement will be made
within 45 days.
