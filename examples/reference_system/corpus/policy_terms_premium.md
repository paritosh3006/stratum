# Arogya Suraksha — Premium, Renewal and Grace

## Premium computation

The premium is computed on the age of the eldest insured member, the sum
insured selected, the zone of residence, and the policy tenure. Premium tables
are published in the prospectus and form part of this contract.

For an individual policy the premium for a member aged 28 with a sum insured of
300000 is 6200 per year. For a member aged 35 with a sum insured of 500000 the
premium is 9400 per year. For a member aged 45 with a sum insured of 500000 the
premium is 14200 per year. For a member aged 52 with a sum insured of 1000000
the premium is 26800 per year. For a member aged 60 with a sum insured of 750000
the premium is 31500 per year. For a member aged 41 with a sum insured of 250000
the premium is 8100 per year.

Goods and services tax is payable in addition to the premium shown above at the
rate applicable on the date of payment.

## Instalment premium

The premium may be paid annually, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly. Where the
premium is paid other than annually, an instalment loading applies. The
instalment facility may be withdrawn if two consecutive instalments remain
unpaid.

Where premium is paid in instalments, cover continues only for the period for
which premium has been received. A claim arising during an unpaid instalment
period is not payable.

## Grace period

A grace period of 30 days is allowed for payment of the renewal premium for
annual policies, and 15 days for instalment premiums. Cover is not available for
any illness contracted during the grace period until the renewal premium has
been received.

If the renewal premium is not received within the grace period, the policy
lapses and all accrued continuity benefits, including credit towards waiting
periods, are forfeited.

## Renewal

The policy is ordinarily renewable for life. The company may decline renewal
only on grounds of fraud, moral hazard, misrepresentation or non-cooperation by
the insured.

On renewal the sum insured may be enhanced subject to underwriting. Waiting
periods apply afresh to the enhanced portion of the sum insured.

## Free look period

The insured may cancel the policy within 15 days of receipt of the policy
document if not satisfied with the terms. The company will refund the premium
after deducting proportionate risk premium for the period of cover, the cost of
medical examination, and stamp duty charges.

## Portability

The insured may port this policy to another insurer at renewal by applying at
least 45 days before the renewal date. Accrued continuity benefits for waiting
periods are carried forward to the extent of the sum insured under the previous
policy.

## Cancellation

The insured may cancel the policy at any time by giving 15 days written notice.
The company will refund premium on a short-period scale as published in the
prospectus, provided no claim has been made during the policy year.
